Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Phone number — required for login and billing
  • Call metadata — duration, timestamps (for billing and safety)
  • IP address — for security and basic geo-routing
  • Payment information — handled by M-Pesa (we never see your M-Pesa PIN)
  • Reports you submit — to track abuse

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not record your video or audio calls
  • We do not store chat content (there is no chat)
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you (phone number)
  • To bill you for calls (balance + call duration)
  • To match you with other users
  • To investigate abuse reports
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Who We Share Data With

  • Supabase — database (stores phone + balance)
  • Railway — server hosting
  • Metered.ca — TURN relay server
  • Safaricom M-Pesa — payment processing
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required

5. Data Retention

We keep your phone number and call metadata for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we remove data within 30 days unless legally required to keep it.

6. Your Rights (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Correction — fix incorrect data
  • Deletion — request account deletion
  • Object — opt out of non-essential processing
  • Complaint — file with the ODPC (odpc.go.ke)

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, secure database access, and industry-standard practices. However, no system is 100% secure — use the Service at your own risk.

8. Children

GlobalCall is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a user is under 18, their account is terminated and data deleted.

9. International Users

Your data may be stored and processed in countries outside Kenya (e.g., US, EU) through our service providers. By using GlobalCall, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept the changes.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: support@globalcall.app
